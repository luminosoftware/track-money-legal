Delete Your Track Money Account

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This page explains how to request permanent deletion of your Track Money account and the application data associated with your account.

Important:

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your deletion has been completed, your Track Money account cannot be recovered.

1. What Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is completed, Track Money will delete the account and associated application data that we maintain for your account.

Depending on the information associated with your account, this may include:

  • Your Track Money account.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your Google-linked application account information.
  • Your email address and account identifier stored for the application.
  • Your stored expense and transaction information.
  • Your application profile information.
  • Other application data associated with your Track Money account.

2. Local Data on Your Device

Track Money may also store certain information locally on your device for application functionality, preferences, offline functionality, or notification scheduling.

Account deletion will address data maintained by Track Money's account and backend services. If information remains locally stored on your device after account deletion, you may need to remove it by following the application's available instructions or by clearing the application's local data through your device settings.

3. Deletion Is Permanent

After your deletion has been confirmed and completed, your Track Money account cannot be restored or recovered.

If you want to use Track Money again after deleting your account, you will need to create a new account.

4. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Track Money account, send an email to the account deletion address below.

For account security, please send the request using the email address associated with your Track Money account whenever possible.

Account deletion email:

luminotechnologyoffice@gmail.com

Please include a request such as:

"Please delete my Track Money account."

5. What Happens After the Request?

  1. We may verify the request when necessary to protect the account and prevent unauthorized deletion requests.
  2. The account deletion process will be initiated.
  3. The Track Money account and associated application data maintained by Track Money will be deleted according to our deletion process.
  4. Once the deletion has been completed, the account cannot be recovered.

6. Firebase and Google Services

Track Money uses Google services including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In, and Google AdMob.

Account and application data stored through Firebase services will be handled as part of the Track Money account deletion process.

Certain information processed by third-party service providers, including advertising or technical information, may be subject to those providers' own deletion mechanisms, retention policies, and applicable legal requirements.

7. Required Legal or Security Retention

In limited circumstances, certain information may need to be retained when required or permitted by applicable law, security requirements, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate obligations.

Any information retained for these purposes will be retained only as permitted or required by the applicable obligation.

8. Privacy Policy

For more information about how Track Money collects, uses, stores, and shares information, please read our Privacy Policy.
