Track Money account deletion

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Lumino Technology provides this page for people who cannot use the Track Money app to request account and associated data deletion.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email us from the address associated with your Track Money account.
  2. Use the subject Track Money account deletion.
  3. In the message, ask us to delete your Track Money account. Do not include your password or financial records.

Email a deletion request

If you cannot send the request from the account email, contact us and we may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership. We will process a verified request and may contact you if clarification is needed.

What deletion removes

After verification, we delete the Firebase Authentication account, its Track Money profile, and associated Firestore records (transactions, categories, budgets, reminders, savings goals, and reports authored by the account). If you complete deletion in the App, it also clears locally stored notes, sync bookkeeping, and its account-deletion recovery marker on that device. Other local preferences, bill-reminder notification ID assignments, and Firestore offline cache may remain on the device. Deletion is permanent. We do not plan routine retention of account records after deletion; limited information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security or fraud-prevention needs.

An email request cannot remotely erase device-only data. Google services may retain limited information under their own policies or legal obligations. Local notes, preferences, and offline cached data may remain until cleared or until an offline device reconnects and processes server deletions. Clear the App’s storage or uninstall it on each device to remove remaining device data.

In the App, account deletion is available by tapping the trash icon beside the Profile heading. It requires an internet connection and Google reauthentication.
