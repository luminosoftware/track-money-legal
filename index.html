Privacy Policy for Track Money

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lumino Technology (“we”, “us”) handles information in Track Money. For questions or privacy requests, email hello@luminotechnology.com.

Information we handle

  • Google account information: Firebase Authentication receives your Google sign-in identity. The App uses your Firebase user identifier and may use your email address and display name to create and manage your account. The App does not request or store your Google password.
  • Financial and app records: information you enter, including income and expense amounts, dates, categories, account or wallet labels, notes, budgets, savings goals, and reminders. Your account settings also include selected currency and monthly calendar.
  • Reports you submit: report type, title, description, submission time, and the signed-in account identifier, email address, and display name available to the App.
  • Service and advertising data: Firebase Authentication processes IP addresses, service/app identifiers, and may process authentication-related user-agent information. Signed-in Firestore requests include your Firebase user identifier. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address, advertising and app/device identifiers, app interactions (including launches and taps), ad views, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. AdMob may share relevant information with Google and its advertising partners to deliver and measure ads, depending on the service and settings.

Track Money does not include Firebase Analytics or a separate crash-reporting SDK. It does not use the connectivity check to build a location history.

How we use and share information

We use account information to authenticate you and associate your records with your account. We use financial and app records to provide tracking, reports, reminders, synchronization, and other features you request. We use submitted reports to investigate the issue or suggestion and respond to you.

Cloud records are processed by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore); Google Sign-In authenticates your account; Google AdMob provides and measures advertisements. We do not sell your personal information or financial records. We do not send your transaction history to AdMob for ad targeting. Reports you submit are stored in Firestore for the developer to review and are not shared with ad providers. These Google services may handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Track Money uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent status, show applicable consent messages, and provide the privacy choices described below. Google’s consent SDK may process technical information needed to determine and present those choices.

When Google requires a privacy options entry point for your location or choices, use Profile → Privacy and cookie settings in the App to review or change your advertising consent choices. You can also manage your device advertising settings through Android settings.

Storage, offline use, and notifications

Cloud Firestore stores your profile and app records and may cache them on your device for offline use. Notes and sync-recovery bookkeeping are stored in a local database. Preferences and daily reminder settings are stored on the device. The App reads your device time zone to schedule local reminders. It also stores a device-only account-deletion recovery marker and bill-reminder notification ID assignments, which can contain your Firebase user identifier and reminder identifiers. Reminder notification schedules are stored locally.

The App requests notification permission when you enable daily reminders. On supported Android devices, exact-alarm access is requested only if you tap “Enable alarms and reminder” in Daily Reminder settings. Internet access is used for sign-in, cloud synchronization, and advertising. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

Retention and deletion

We retain cloud account and app records while your account is active. You can delete your account in the App by tapping the trash icon beside the Profile heading. The flow requires an internet connection and recent Google reauthentication. It deletes the Firebase Authentication account, account profile, and associated Firestore records (including reports authored by the account). In-app deletion also clears locally stored notes and the account-deletion recovery marker and sync bookkeeping on that device. Other local preferences, bill-reminder ID assignments, and Firestore offline cache may remain on the device as described below.

If you cannot access the App, visit our Track Money account deletion page to request cloud account and record deletion by email. An email request cannot remotely erase device-only data. Local preferences, notes, and offline caches may remain until cleared or until an offline device reconnects and processes server deletions. Clear the App’s storage or uninstall it on each device to remove remaining local data. Google services may retain limited information under their own policies or legal obligations. We do not plan routine retention of account records after a completed deletion; limited retention may occur when required by law or for legitimate security or fraud-prevention needs.

Security

Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules restrict account records to the signed-in account and report access to the report author. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect the Google account you use with Track Money.

Children

Track Money is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information inappropriately.

Changes

We may update this policy when Track Money or its data practices change. The current version will be available in the App and on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
