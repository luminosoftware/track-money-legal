Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Track Money, developed by Lumino Technology, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

By using Track Money, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Track Money collects or processes information that is necessary to provide account authentication, expense tracking, data synchronization, notifications, advertising, application functionality, and security.

Account and Authentication Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In, Track Money may receive information associated with your Google account that is made available through the authentication process. This may include your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and other basic account information permitted by the sign-in process.

Firebase Authentication is used to authenticate and manage your Track Money account.

Financial and Expense Information

When you use Track Money, you may provide financial and expense information such as transaction amounts, transaction types, categories, accounts, dates, notes, and other information that you choose to enter into the application.

This information is used to provide expense tracking, reporting, organization, synchronization, and related application features.

Information Stored on Your Device

Track Money may store certain application information locally on your device. Local storage may be used to support application functionality, preferences, locally stored information, notification scheduling, and other features.

Depending on how you use the application, locally stored information may include application preferences, locally cached expense-related information, notification settings, and other data required for the application to function correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected or provided through the application may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your application account.
  • Authenticating you through Google Sign-In.
  • Storing and synchronizing your expense information.
  • Providing expense tracking and reporting features.
  • Maintaining application security and preventing abuse.
  • Providing local notifications that you enable.
  • Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Maintaining application functionality and reliability.
  • Improving the application and its features.
  • Responding to support or account-related requests.

3. Firebase Services

Track Money uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Firebase Authentication is used to authenticate users and manage application accounts.

Cloud Firestore is used to store and synchronize application data, including expense and transaction information associated with your Track Money account.

Firebase services may process technical information required to provide their services, which may include information such as IP address, application information, device information, and other technical or diagnostic information depending on the Firebase services being used.

Firebase processes information according to Google's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

4. Google Sign-In

Track Money uses Google Sign-In to allow users to authenticate their accounts.

When you sign in with Google, Google may provide the application with account information permitted by the authentication process, such as your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information.

We use this information to create, authenticate, and manage your Track Money account.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Track Money uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob and related advertising technologies may process certain information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, application information, and information relating to advertising interactions, depending on the advertising configuration and applicable settings.

Advertising information may be used to provide, measure, maintain, protect, and improve advertising services and to help prevent fraud or abuse.

Personalized advertising may be provided where permitted by applicable settings, consent requirements, and Google's policies.

We do not use your expense or financial information to sell your personal information or financial information to advertisers.

6. Device and Network Information

Track Money and the third-party services used by the application may process technical information about your device and network connection when necessary to provide application functionality, security, advertising, or other services.

Depending on the feature or third-party service being used, this information may include:

  • Device model and manufacturer information.
  • Operating-system information and version.
  • Application version and technical information.
  • IP address and network-related information.
  • Advertising or device identifiers.
  • Connectivity and network status.
  • Technical information required for security and service operation.

This information is not used by Track Money to sell your financial information.

7. Permissions

Track Money may request certain device permissions when a feature requires them. Permissions are requested only for functionality supported by the application and may depend on the operating system and device.

You can manage applicable permissions through your device's operating-system settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related application feature from functioning correctly.

8. Local Notifications

Track Money may provide local notifications for features that you enable within the application, such as reminders or scheduled notifications.

Notification scheduling information may be stored locally on your device so that the application can schedule and deliver the notifications you have requested.

You can manage notification permissions through your device's settings.

9. Local Storage and Offline Functionality

Track Money uses local device storage to support application functionality. Depending on the feature, this may include locally stored application data, preferences, cached information, or notification scheduling information.

Local storage is used to improve application functionality and may allow certain information to remain available on the device when network connectivity is unavailable.

Information stored locally on your device may remain on the device until it is removed by the application, cleared through the device or application settings, or otherwise deleted as part of the account deletion process where applicable.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or your financial and expense information.

Information may nevertheless be processed by third-party service providers that are necessary to operate the application. These may include Google services such as Firebase, Google Sign-In, and Google AdMob.

These providers may process information according to their respective terms, policies, and privacy practices.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Track Money.

Data transmitted to services used by the application may be protected using security technologies and protections provided by those services.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Account Deletion

Track Money provides users with a way to permanently delete their account.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once the deletion has been confirmed and completed, the account cannot be recovered.

When an account deletion request is completed, the Track Money account and associated application data maintained by Track Money will be deleted, subject to information that we may be legally required or legitimately permitted to retain for purposes such as security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other applicable obligations.

Where third-party service providers process information on behalf of Track Money, deletion requests will be handled in accordance with the capabilities, terms, and applicable retention requirements of those services.

Information stored locally on your device may also need to be removed from the device where applicable. Users should follow the account deletion instructions provided by Track Money.

To request account deletion, visit:

Request Account Deletion

13. Data Retention

We retain account and expense information for as long as necessary to provide the application's services and while your account remains active.

When your account is permanently deleted, associated application data maintained by Track Money will be deleted according to our account deletion process, except where retention is required or permitted by applicable law, security requirements, fraud prevention, or other legitimate obligations.

14. Children's Privacy

Track Money is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application inappropriately, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

15. Third-Party Services

Track Money uses third-party services to provide certain application functionality. These services currently include:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Google AdMob

These providers may process information according to their respective terms, policies, and privacy practices.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, legal requirements, or data-processing practices.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or account deletion, please contact:

Lumino Technology
